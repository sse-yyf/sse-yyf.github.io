I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Stockholm School of Economics and a member of Lab².

I use learning models, experimental methods, and machine learning to study how people make decisions in dynamic games. I am also interested in meta-analysis and the replicability of economics studies. Recently, I used network methods to study intergenerational inequality.
